## Introduction

This charter describes the operations of the CNCF Research User Group. The
group focuses on discussing and sharing cloud native practices for research
organizations, such as universities, national laboratories, HPC centers, and
scientific collaborations. Other topics of interest are building and operating
cloud native infrastructure for research computing.

This user group (UG) produces supporting material and best practices for
research end users. It is also a channel for research user feedback to CNCF
projects and related groups (see [Cross-group relationships](#cross-group-relationships)).

## Mission

The mission of the CNCF Research User Group is to:

- Act as a central channel for discussing and advancing research computing using
  cloud native technologies.
- Collect current practices and identify gaps in the cloud native ecosystem
  for scientific workloads.
- Develop informational resources, including guides, reference architectures,
  case studies, and white papers, on best practices and trade-offs focused on
  research use cases.
- Identify useful CNCF projects for research use cases and provide feedback
  to these projects.
- Collect and share information about gaps and possibilities for improvement
  within the full CNCF ecosystem.

## Areas considered in Scope

The CNCF Research User Group focuses on the following topics as they apply to
research and scientific computing:

- Batch, HTC, and HPC Workloads on Kubernetes
  - _Job scheduling, queueing, fair share, quotas, and MPI or tightly coupled
    workloads._
- HPC in Cloud Native Landscapes
  - _Integrating with traditional HPC schedulers (e.g. Slurm), and containerized
  workloads on HPC systems._
- Scientific Workflows and Interactive Environments
  - _Workflow engines, reproducibility, data provenance, notebooks, and science
    gateways._
- Federated and Multi-Institution Infrastructure
  - _Federated identities, multi-cluster setups, and shared research platforms
    across institutions._

The group will:

- Develop best practices for research organizations using cloud native
technologies.
- Encourage collaboration between research institutions and CNCF projects.
- Propose new initiatives when gaps are identified.

For CNCF projects, the Research User Group interacts mainly with
workload-focused projects and groups, for example:

- Providing research use cases and requirements to batch and scheduling
  efforts (e.g. Kueue, Volcano).

## Areas considered out of Scope

Anything not explicitly listed in the scope above. Examples include:

- Generic HPC/AI/ML work outside research.
- Traditional HPC topics that do not involve cloud native technologies.
- Research data policy work and research data licensing.
- Individual user support.
