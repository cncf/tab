## Purpose of the group

With telecom workloads moving toward cloud native architectures there is a need for a neutral space to discuss the
expectations of operators, implementation best practices of vendors, gaps, architectural evolution and requirements of
the cloud native community. The purpose of this group is to provide a neutral space where all of these discussions can
happen in a structured form and the conclusions can be shared with the cloud native community and the CNCF projects. The
Group plans to continuously seek alignment with other CNCF initiatives which are related to the telecoms, like other
End User groups, the AI Conformance program or DRANET and possibly communities outside the CNCF such as Sylva or the
LF-Networking

Based on the group's interest the group might target the creation of a Domain Reference Architecture for Telecoms and
would promote the existing Reference Architectures. 

The group plans to have regular bi-weekly meetings open to anyone.

## Scope

### Scope of the group

The scope of the Telecom User Group is to represent the telecom industry in the CNCF ecosystem, identify gaps in CNCF
projects relevant for the telecom industry, share experiences, feedback and ideas among telecom actors. Analyze and
provide feedback of ongoing architectural work in the CNCF community. Optionally to build a Domain Reference
Architecture for Telecoms. Serve as a liaison between the LF Networking projects, like CNTi, Sylva and the CNCF.

### Not in scope of the group

At the moment the group does not scope the creation of whitepapers and especially does not target to implement any
telecom variants for cloud native technology. 

## Governance

The group has two (2) co-chairs each of them have two year terms. The co-chairs of the team are responsible for keeping
a healthy agenda, organize the calls for the group and make sure that all group members can express their opinions. 

Any member of the group can be nominated for co-chairs. The group co-chairs are elected by the members of the group and
approved by the CNCF End User TAB. The terms overlap in a way that in 2026 and every even year one (1) co-chair is
elected and in 2027 and every future odd year the other one (1)  co-chair is elected. 

The group annually monitors its participation and when the number of regular participants will become less than 4 the
group will dismantle itself and report the event to the CNCF End User TAB.

The initial co-chairs are two volunteers from the proposal group for the End User Group names during the creation
process.
